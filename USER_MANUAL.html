1. System Overview & Architecture

The Daily Pearls PH Order Management System (OMS) is custom-engineered to handle the dynamic sales operations of luxury fine jewelry, cultured pearls, custom mounts, and Moissanite stones.

📦 Multi-Channel Order Flow

Supports Regular Live Selling Reservations, Custom Client Sourcing (Pasabuy), and Cash-on-Delivery (COD) orders with line-item management.

💳 Multi-Payment & Evidence

Handles partial downpayments, remaining balance tracking, and immutable audit logs with receipt proof attachments.

💎 Moissanite Special Inventory

Dedicated SKU registry, carat/setting specifications, cost accounting, and profit tracking per stone.

📊 Employee Commissions & Payouts

Attribution of assisted sales to team members with real-time settlement tracking (Paid vs. Unpaid).

3. Logging In & Navigation

3.1 Authentication

  1. Open your web browser and navigate to /login.
  2. Enter your registered corporate Email Address and Password.
  3. Click Sign In to enter the secure dashboard session.
ℹ️
Security Note: Sessions utilize encrypted HTTP-only cookies. When finishing your shift on shared computers, always click Sign Out in the user menu.

4. Dashboard & Business Analytics

The main dashboard displays high-priority operational metrics:

  • Total Revenue & Sales: Aggregated completed collections and total invoiced values.
  • Active Pipeline: Counters for Reserved, Paid, In Transit (Shipped), and Claimed packages.
  • RTO Watch: Direct indicator of failed deliveries requiring immediate customer follow-up.

5. Orders & Reservation Lifecycle

5.1 Reservation Types

  • Regular: Standard items reserved during live selling streams or direct inquiries.
  • Pasabuy: Custom-sourced or bespoke jewelry items procured specifically for a customer.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Orders where the full amount or remaining balance is collected by the courier.

5.2 Creating a New Reservation

  1. Go to Orders > Reserved and click + Add Reservation.
  2. Input Customer FB Name, Recipient Name, Phone Number, and complete Shipping Address.
  3. In the Order Items (Bulk Ordering) section:
    • Click + Add Item to append multiple line items.
    • Choose source: Manual Item or Moissanite Catalog.
    • Set quantity and unit price. Line totals compute automatically.
  4. Set Discount (if applicable) and Shipping Fee.
  5. Enter the initial Downpayment (DP) and choose the Payment Method.
  6. Click Save Reservation. A unique invoice number is automatically assigned.

5.4 Shipping, Delivery & RTO Operations

  • Ship Order: Open the order details, click Ship Order, select courier (e.g., LBC, J&T, Flash, Grab), and enter the tracking number.
  • Mark Claimed: Once delivery confirmation is received, click Mark Claimed.
  • Mark as RTO: If returned by courier, specify the RTO reason (e.g., Unreachable, Refused Delivery) and notes.
  • Reship: Under Orders > RTO, click Reship Order to assign a new tracking number and restart transit.

6. Payment Processing & Evidence

The system supports split payments (initial downpayment followed by balance payments):

  1. Open the target order and click Record Payment.
  2. Choose kind: Downpayment or Balance.
  3. Select the channel: GCash, Maya, BDO, BPI, UnionBank, COD, Cash, Remittance.
  4. Enter the Reference Number and upload the proof of payment receipt image.
  5. Click Save Payment. The invoice summary and remaining balance update in real time.

8. Collections & Cash Reconciliation

Access the Collections tab to view a centralized ledger of all funds collected across all channels. Filter by date, payment method, or customer name to conduct fast daily cash counts and bank deposit reconciliations.

9. Moissanite Jewelry Inventory & Sales

  • SKU Catalog (/moissanite/sku): Manage custom stones, settings (Solitaire, Halo, 6-Prong), carat weight, cost prices, and retail prices.
  • Sold Ledger (/moissanite/sold): Automatic tracking of sold stones linked directly to customer invoice numbers, showing realized revenue and gross profit margins.

10. Products & Category Management

Maintain your core product catalog (South Sea Pearls, Freshwater Pearls, Akoya, 14k/18k Mounts, Chains, Earrings) under Products and Categories. Toggle active/inactive states to control catalog availability.

11. Employee Commission Tracking

Located under Commission & Assistance (Admin/Owner only):

  1. Click + Add Record to log a sales assistant's contribution to a closed order.
  2. The system tracks total monthly commission by staff member, order sales volume, and settlement status.
  3. Use the action menu to mark items as Paid upon salary or commission payout.

12. Customer Directory

The Customers view provides a complete customer database with contact numbers, delivery addresses, Facebook handles, and cumulative lifetime purchase history.

13. Reports, Analytics & Exports

Generate high-level and detailed monthly performance summaries under Reports:

  • Monthly gross and net sales, shipping fees collected, discounts applied.
  • Order conversion rates, RTO percentages, and claim rates.
  • CSV / Excel Export: Click Export CSV on any data table across Orders, Collections, or Commissions for external accounting spreadsheets.

14. Account Management & FAQ

14.1 Managing Staff Accounts

Administrators can create new accounts, assign roles (Staff, Admin, Viewer), or deactivate departed personnel under Accounts.

14.2 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I handle a customer who paid via GCash first and will pay the balance in Cash?
A: Enter the initial GCash payment as Downpayment. When the remaining amount is collected upon delivery, open the order and click Record Payment with Cash as the method.

Q: Can I edit an order after saving?
A: Yes, click the Edit button in the reservation view. Line items and shipping fees can be updated anytime before dispatch.

Q: How do I print the invoice as a PDF file?
A: Navigate to the order invoice page, click Print Invoice (or press Ctrl+P on your keyboard), and select Save as PDF as the destination printer.
