3 Automatic Repeat Customer Compilation

When a customer claims multiple items at different times during a live selling session, the system prevents creating duplicate split orders by automatically merging their claims into a single invoice.

⚡
How Auto-Compilation Works:
When you type the buyer's FB Name or Customer Name in Add Reservation, the system checks for any open active reservation (status = "reserved"). If found, it displays an amber notice: "Active reservation found! Existing items loaded into this form."
1
Type Customer Name: Start typing Maria S. or Maria Santos.
2
Existing Order Loads: All previously claimed jewelry items appear automatically in the item table.
3
Add New Items: Click + Add Item to add the new piece the customer just mined or claimed.
4
Save Consolidated Order: Click Save Reservation. The system updates the original invoice without creating duplicate records.

4 Creating a Reservation (Step-by-Step)

1
Order Type: Choose Regular (live stream pearls/jewelry), Pasabuy (custom mount/sourcing), or COD.
2
Customer Details: Enter Facebook Name, Full Name, Contact Number, and Delivery Address.
3
Line Items: Select item from catalog or type custom item name, quantity, and unit price.
4
Financials: Enter any agreed Discount and courier Shipping Fee.
5
Downpayment (DP): If the customer deposited an initial advance, enter the DP amount and select the payment channel (GCash, BDO, BPI, Maya, Cash).
6
Confirm: Click Save Reservation. Click the customer's FB name in any table to open the invoice.

2
Select Kind: Choose Downpayment (initial deposit) or Balance (settling balance).
3

7 Shipping, Claiming & Handling Returns (RTO)

📦 How to Ship an Order
Open the order, click Ship Order, select courier (LBC, J&T, Flash Express, Grab/Lalamove), and type the Tracking Number. Status moves to Shipped.
✅ How to Mark as Claimed
When courier status confirms package delivery, open Orders > Shipped and click Mark Claimed.
🔄 How to Handle Return to Origin (RTO) & Reship
If delivery fails, click Mark as RTO and select the reason. To reship after coordinating with the customer, open Orders > RTO and click Reship Order with a new tracking code.

12 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I handle a customer who claims items across different live stream days?
A: As long as their reservation is in Reserved status, typing their name in Add Reservation will load their open order. You can add the new items directly to save shipping and merge their invoice.
Q: Can I record multiple payments for the same order?
A: Yes. Use Record Payment as many times as needed (e.g. ₱500 GCash deposit + ₱2,500 BDO balance). Each payment is logged with its own receipt screenshot.
Q: Why can't staff or admin accounts see the Commission or Reports pages?
A: By design, financial analytics and employee commission data are restricted exclusively to Developer and Owner roles to maintain business and financial confidentiality.
